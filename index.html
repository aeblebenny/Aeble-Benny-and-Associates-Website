Office information

About the office.

The office of Aeble Benny & Associates, based in Ernakulam, Kerala, deals with legal matters relating to criminal law, including criminal proceedings, bail, appeals and preventive detention. The office also deals with matters involving cyber law, including legal issues arising from online fraud, cybercrime, digital evidence and identity misuse, in accordance with applicable law and professional obligations.

The areas of legal work also include family and matrimonial matters, such as divorce, maintenance, custody and related disputes, as well as property and real estate matters involving title issues, agreements and property-related disputes. Civil litigation matters may include contractual disputes, recovery proceedings, injunctions and other civil proceedings before competent forums.

The office also undertakes legal work relating to corporate and commercial matters, contracts, compliance and legal documentation, including the preparation and review of legal notices, agreements, affidavits, petitions and deeds. The nature of each matter is considered in accordance with its facts, applicable law and the professional duties governing advocates.
